Key Retrieval Tutorial (work.ink)

Follow these steps to obtain your access key via the work.ink link-gate.

Get the Key Link

Load the Da7mu Hub script in your executor. When prompted for a key, click the **'Get Key'** button. This will copy the special **work.ink** key retrieval link to your clipboard.

Pass the work.ink Gates

Paste the link into your browser. You must successfully complete the steps on the work.ink page, usually by clicking **"Free Access with Ads"** and following the subsequent instructions (e.g., waiting, or interacting with ads/articles) to unlock the final page.

Retrieve and Copy the Key

After successfully passing the link-gate, you will be redirected to the page containing your unique access key. **Copy the entire key text.** The key will look similar to this: DA7MU-HUB-KEY-1A2B-3C4D

Paste Key and Execute

Return to your executor. Paste the copied key into the key input field and click **Execute** or **Submit**. The script will verify the key and unlock all features for you to use.
